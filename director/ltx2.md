# Model guide: LTX-2 / LTX-2.3 (Lightricks) — video with native audio

Source: official LTX-2 prompting guide (docs.ltx.video/api-documentation/prompting-guide).

## Structure
One flowing paragraph, 4–8 sentences, present tense. In this order:
1. Shot — scale and cinematography ("Medium close-up, shallow depth of field").
2. Scene — lighting, color palette, textures, atmosphere.
3. Action — the core action as a sequence from start to end.
4. Character — age, hair, clothing, distinctive features; emotion through visible physical cues.
5. Camera — how and when it moves ("slow dolly in", "handheld tracking", "static frame").
6. Audio — ambient sound, music, speech or singing.

## Dialogue and lip-sync
- Put spoken words in quotation marks: He says, "Welcome to my island!"
- Give delivery: warm, cheerful voice; whisper; shout; energetic announcer.
- Name language/accent if not English.
- When a voice track is attached (lip-sync workflow): state that the character speaks from the very first frame,
  mouth and lips moving in sync with the voice, facing the camera. Otherwise the mouth often opens late.

## Detail
- Close-ups need more detail than wide shots.
- Short clips (2–5 s): one action, one camera move.

## Avoid
- Emotion labels without visible cues ("sad", "confused") — show it instead (shoulders drop, eyes widen).
- Readable text, signs, logos.
- Complex physics, chaotic motion, crowds of characters.
- Conflicting light sources.
- Overloaded, overly long prompts.

## Strengths
Cinematic single-subject moments, atmosphere (fog, rain, golden hour), clear camera language,
stylized looks (painterly, noir, pixel, toy/3D), rim light and backlight.

## Example (image + voice, 3 s)
Medium close-up of a cheerful blocky toy-style islander standing by a crackling campfire on a sunny beach,
warm golden-hour light and soft sea haze behind him. From the very first frame he talks straight to the camera,
his mouth and lips moving in sync with his voice, eyebrows lifting as he smiles. He gestures toward the fire
with his free hand while the axe rests on his shoulder. The camera holds a static frame with a gentle slow push in.
Audio: his warm, upbeat voice, soft waves and the crackle of the fire.
